# Business Insights — Olist E-Commerce Analysis

## Executive Summary

The Olist e-commerce analysis reveals strong revenue expansion between 2017 and 2018, but also highlights significant weaknesses in customer retention and delivery experience.

Revenue increased by 157.2% between January–August 2017 and January–August 2018, while average order value remained almost unchanged. This indicates that growth was primarily driven by a substantial increase in order volume rather than customers spending more per transaction.

Customer retention is the largest structural weakness. Approximately 97% of analyzed customers made only one purchase, while only about 3% returned for another purchase.

Product performance is concentrated around a few major categories, with Beauty & Health generating the highest revenue at approximately $1.259M.

Delivery performance also has a clear relationship with customer satisfaction. On-time orders received an average review score of 4.29/5 compared with only 2.57/5 for late orders — a 1.73-point gap.

Overall, the business demonstrates strong demand and growth potential, but improving retention and delivery reliability could be critical for sustaining long-term growth.

# 1. Revenue Growth

### Finding

| Period | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Jan–Aug 2017 | $2.54M |
| Jan–Aug 2018 | $6.53M |
| Growth | +157.2% |

Orders increased from 18,400 to 47,322 during the same period, also representing approximately 157.2% growth.

However, Average Order Value remained almost unchanged:

| Metric | Jan–Aug 2017 | Jan–Aug 2018 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average Order Value | $137.99 | $138.02 |

### Business Interpretation

Revenue growth was primarily volume-driven rather than driven by higher customer spending per order.

This suggests that the platform successfully expanded transaction activity, but there may still be opportunities to increase customer value through cross-selling, bundles, premium products and repeat purchases.

### Business Implication

The next phase of growth should not rely only on acquiring more orders.

The business should focus on increasing:

- Repeat purchase frequency
- Customer lifetime value
- Cross-category purchases
- Average order value

---

# 2. Monthly Revenue Trend

### Finding

Revenue increased substantially between 2017 and 2018 across every comparable month.

The strongest growth occurred in January:

- January 2017: $120K
- January 2018: $950K
- YoY growth: +689.9%

By August, the YoY growth rate had normalized to +48.9%.

The strongest 2018 month was April at approximately $997K.

### Business Interpretation

The business experienced rapid expansion from the early 2017 baseline, followed by a gradual normalization of growth.

The important point is that 2018 monthly revenue remained substantially above 2017 levels even after growth rates began moderating.

### Business Implication

Management should distinguish between:

- Absolute revenue growth
- Percentage growth from a low base
- Sustainable underlying growth

Future performance should be evaluated using both revenue and order-volume trends rather than YoY percentage growth alone.

# 4. Customer Retention

### Finding

| Customer Type | Customers | Orders | Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| One-time | 92,507 | 92,507 | $12.83M |
| Repeat | 2,913 | 6,159 | $763K |
| Total | 95,420 | 98,666 | $13.59M |

Approximately 97% of analyzed customers were one-time purchasers, while only approximately 3% were repeat customers.

Repeat customers generated more than one order on average, demonstrating the value of retaining existing customers.

### Business Interpretation

Customer acquisition appears much stronger than customer retention.

The platform is successfully generating transactions, but a very large proportion of customers do not return.

This represents the clearest strategic opportunity identified in the analysis.

### Business Implication

The business should develop post-purchase retention initiatives such as:

- Personalized follow-up campaigns
- Category-based recommendations
- Repeat-purchase incentives
- Cross-selling recommendations
- Loyalty or rewards programs
- Targeted offers for high-value one-time customers

The objective should be to convert high-potential one-time customers into repeat purchasers.

# 5. High-Value Customer Opportunity

### Finding

The highest-spending customer spent approximately $13,440 in a single order.

The second-highest spender generated approximately $7,388 across two orders.

### Business Interpretation

Although most customers are one-time buyers, there are customers with significantly higher transaction values.

This suggests that customer segmentation could uncover valuable groups that deserve differentiated retention strategies.

### Business Implication

Instead of treating every customer equally, the business could prioritize:

- High-value one-time customers
- Repeat customers
- Customers purchasing premium categories
- Customers with high order values

This could improve marketing efficiency and customer lifetime value.

# 7. Payment Behavior

### Finding

Credit cards dominated payment activity:

- 76,795 transactions
- $12.54M payment value
- $163.32 average transaction

Boleto was the second-largest payment method:

- 19,784 transactions
- $2.87M payment value
- $145.03 average transaction

### Business Interpretation

Credit card payments are the dominant payment channel.

Installment behavior also indicates that customers use financing to support larger purchases.

### Business Implication

The platform can potentially use installment messaging and payment flexibility to encourage higher-value purchases, particularly in categories with high average order values.

# 8. Delivery Performance

### Finding

Average delivery time was approximately 12.5 days.

Delivery performance:

- On-time: 91.89%
- Late: 8.11%

### Business Interpretation

Overall on-time performance is strong, but approximately 1 in every 12 orders was late.

Even a relatively small late-delivery segment can have a disproportionate impact on customer satisfaction.

---

# 9. Delivery & Customer Satisfaction

### Finding

| Delivery Status | Average Review |
|---|---:|
| On Time | 4.29 / 5 |
| Late | 2.57 / 5 |

Difference:

**1.73 points**

### Business Interpretation

The relationship between delivery experience and customer satisfaction is one of the clearest findings in the analysis.

Customers receiving orders on time rated their experience substantially higher than customers whose orders were late.

### Business Implication

Reducing late deliveries should be treated as a customer-experience initiative, not simply a logistics KPI.

Potential actions include:

- Identifying sellers with recurring delays
- Monitoring delivery performance by region
- Improving estimated delivery accuracy
- Investigating long delivery-time orders
- Prioritizing high-risk shipments

# 10. Overall Business Priorities

Based on the analysis, four priorities stand out.

## Priority 1 — Improve Customer Retention

Approximately 97% of customers were one-time buyers.

**Objective:** Increase repeat purchase rate and customer lifetime value.

---

## Priority 2 — Reduce Late Deliveries

Late orders received an average rating of only 2.57/5 versus 4.29/5 for on-time orders.

**Objective:** Reduce the late-order rate and protect customer satisfaction.

---

## Priority 3 — Grow Customer Value

Revenue increased by 157.2%, while AOV remained essentially flat.

**Objective:** Increase revenue per customer through cross-selling, bundles, premium products and repeat purchases.

---

## Priority 4 — Strengthen High-Performing Categories & Sellers

Beauty & Health, Watches & Gifts and Bed/Bath/Table were among the strongest revenue categories.

**Objective:** Focus inventory, seller quality and promotional efforts around proven demand.

---

# Final Takeaway

The Olist marketplace demonstrates strong transaction growth and clear product-market demand.

However, the analysis suggests that the next stage of growth should focus less on simply generating more transactions and more on improving the quality and value of existing customer relationships.

The biggest opportunities are:

1. **Convert one-time buyers into repeat customers**
2. **Reduce late deliveries**
3. **Increase customer value without relying solely on order-volume growth**
4. **Prioritize high-performing categories and sellers**
5. **Use customer and seller segmentation to target interventions**

The central business message is:

> **Olist has demonstrated that it can generate demand; the next opportunity is turning that demand into stronger retention, higher customer value and more reliable customer experiences.**
